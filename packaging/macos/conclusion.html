Woodpile is installed and listening

It receives syslog on UDP and TCP port 514 and runs as a background service, also after a restart.

Open http://127.0.0.1:8514/ on this Mac to see the welcome page.

Logs and settings: /Library/Application Support/Woodpile
Service log: /Library/Logs/Woodpile/woodpile.log

To uninstall: sudo /usr/local/share/woodpile/uninstall.sh (add --delete-logs to delete the logs too).
